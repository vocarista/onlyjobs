## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-clarity, professional, and friction-free visual language tailored for modern job hunting, recruitment intelligence, and talent pipelines. The aesthetic strikes a careful balance between high-fidelity wireframing and pristine product interface: clean, systematic, structured, and airy.

### Visual Personality
- **Airy & Uncluttered:** Extensive breathable space with deliberate structural framing that eliminates cognitive fatigue during dense data scanning.
- **Architectural & Systematic:** Rooted in clean borders, refined dividers, and subtle surface elevations rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Confident & Focused:** Cool slate neutrals accented by a purposeful, calming indigo highlight actionable touchpoints without overwhelming editorial content.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Job seekers, hiring managers, and recruiters who balance multiple applications, dense candidate pipelines, and fast-paced screening tasks.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires clarity, trust, calmness, and deliberate momentum. The interface recedes cleanly into the background, prioritizing typography, metadata, and task completion.

## Colors

The palette leverages a pristine slate architecture with subtle tonal shifts, crisp neutral strokes, and a disciplined indigo-led accent system.

### Core Canvas & Neutral Structure
- **Canvas Base:** `#ffffff` (Pure White) serves as the primary card and container base to ensure crisp contrast.
- **Canvas Substrate:** `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) forms the page foundation, offering an optical separation from interactive cards.
- **Surface Muted:** `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100) provides subtle fills for hovered elements, badges, and read-only inputs.
- **Border Default:** `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200) serves as the universal wireframe boundary stroke.
- **Border Strong:** `#cbd5e1` (Slate 300) indicates active inputs, divider accents, and hovered card perimeters.

### Typography & Content Grayscale
- **Content Primary:** `#0f172a` (Slate 900) for high-contrast titles, headers, and essential metrics.
- **Content Secondary:** `#334155` (Slate 700) for body copy, label descriptors, and form fields.
- **Content Muted:** `#64748b` (Slate 500) for secondary metadata, timestamps, and input placeholders.
- **Content Subtle:** `#94a3b8` (Slate 400) for de-emphasized icons, inactive steps, and keyboard shortcuts.

### Accent & Functional Status System
- **Primary Accent (`#4f46e5`):** Used for primary buttons, focus rings, selected pipeline stages, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary Dark Accent (`#0f172a`):** Used for solid chip tags, high-priority status badges, and authoritative actions.
- **Info Accent (`#0284c7`):** Signals open roles, informational callouts, and external link indicators.
- **Success Accent (`#059669` / soft fill `#ecfdf5`):** Expresses active offers, matched resumes, and verified company credentials.
- **Warning Accent (`#d97706` / soft fill `#fffbeb`):** Expresses urgent deadlines, missing portfolio items, and expiring listings.
- **Review Accent (`#7c3aed` / soft fill `#f5f3ff`):** Signals technical reviews, interview scheduling, and premium candidate spotlights.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headers and display banners with **Inter** for dense transactional UI data, forms, and analytical metadata.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings approachable modernism and subtle geometric warmth to brand statements, company names, and salary callouts without distracting from utilitarian tasks.
- **Inter** ensures crystal-clear readability for fine job descriptions, tabular salary breakdowns, and multi-tier applicant status pills across diverse display resolutions.
- Numerical figures in compensation and application metrics should use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain alignment across comparison grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a structured, responsive 12-column fluid grid built on an 8px vertical cadence. It prioritizes wide spatial rhythm, deliberate content breathing room, and wireframe-like structural order.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, max-width container of `1280px`, `2rem` outer margins, and `1.5rem` gutters. Ideal for 3-pane workflows (Navigation Sidebar, Job Pipeline / Search Listing, Detail / Application Drawer).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer margins, and `1rem` gutters. Navigation collapses to a slim icon rail; detail views open as an overlay sheet or dedicated secondary page.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer margins, and `0.75rem` gutters. Content stacks into unified, single-column feeds with sticky action bars anchored at the bottom edge.

### Layout Philosophy
- Spacing follows an intentional distribution: internal component groupings rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm`, component padding leverages `space-md` to `space-lg`, and major structural sections use `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark or heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows, maintaining an airy "clean slate" aesthetic that reflects structured wireframe precision.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main backdrop (`#f8fafc`). Content panels lay flush with no shadow, bounded only by a 1px border (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Grid Containers):** Applied to job cards, filter bars, and applicant rows. Pure white background (`#ffffff`), 1px border stroke (`#e2e8f0`), paired with an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** Applied when hovering interactive cards, open dropdown menus, and popovers. Elevated with a refined slate diffusion: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and border stroke shifted to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Slide-over Drawers):** Applied to candidate profiles, full application reviews, and action modals. Composed of an ambient lift: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a semi-transparent slate wash backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.3)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded corner language (Level 2: `0.5rem` / 8px default) to communicate modern refinement while preserving the crisp, engineered discipline of wireframes.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Base Surfaces & Inputs (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Buttons, search fields, text inputs, dropdown toggles, and table cells.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px):** Job listing cards, profile containers, and summary analytics blocks.
- **Drawers & Modals (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px):** Major overlay surfaces, onboarding modals, and desktop floating bars.
- **Pills & Status Badges (`9999px`):** Status indicators, tags, salary badges, and avatar counters use full pill shapes to contrast against rectilinear card geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#4f46e5` background, pure white text, 8px corner radius, zero outline, subtle inner highlight. Focus state displays a 2px offset ring in `#4f46e5`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#ffffff`, 1px border in `#e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`. Hover transitions to background `#f8fafc` and border `#cbd5e1`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** No border or fill in rest state; text `#334155`. Background becomes `#f1f5f9` on hover.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Standard height: 40px (compact) and 48px (hero search).
- Background: `#ffffff`, encased in a 1px `#e2e8f0` border.
- Placeholder text in `#64748b`. Focus state replaces the border with `#4f46e5` accompanied by an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12)` glow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Skill / Tag Chips:** Height 28px, rounded-full, `#f1f5f9` fill, `#334155` text, 1px `#e2e8f0` border. Hover triggers `#e2e8f0` fill.
- **Status Pills:** Soft tinted fills with high-contrast text:
  - *Applied/Interview:* Background `#eff6ff`, text `#0284c7`, border `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.
  - *Offered/Active:* Background `#ecfdf5`, text `#059669`, border `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`.
  - *Urgent/Reviewing:* Background `#fffbeb`, text `#d97706`, border `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`.

### Cards (Job Listing & Pipeline Stages)
- Clean `#ffffff` canvas with 1px `#e2e8f0` stroke and `1rem` corner rounding.
- Interior layout incorporates an airy 20px padding (`space-lg`), separating company icon, title hierarchy, tag row, and salary/location footer with clean 1px divider lines.
- Hover brings a crisp `#cbd5e1` boundary stroke and subtle Level 2 elevation lift.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with 1.5px `#cbd5e1` border.
- Checked state uses `#4f46e5` background with an inverted crisp white checkmark or center pip. Focus adds a 2px indigo aura.

### Lists & Tables
- Border-bottom separated rows using `#f1f5f9`, featuring alternating column whitespace rather than zebra stripes.
- Row hover triggers smooth `#f8fafc` wash across the full target row with interactive pointer feedback.
