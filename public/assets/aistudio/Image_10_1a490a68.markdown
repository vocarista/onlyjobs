## Brand & Style

The design system embodies calm authority, analytical focus, and modern executive polish for job seekers tracking pipeline stages and rehearsing interviews. The user interface prioritizes clarity under pressure: job hunting and high-stakes interviews are inherently stressful, demanding a digital environment that projects control, quiet confidence, and tactical readiness.

Drawing from sleek modern minimalism infused with tactical dashboard ergonomics, the visual identity rejects noisy decorative trends, deep saturated oceanic blues, and stark pitch-black voids. Instead, it leverages deep gunmetal slate, cool charcoal strata, and precision-tuned indigo/violet focal points. 

Key attributes:
- **Tone:** Professional, composed, razor-sharp, analytical.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep cool workspace with selective luminous highlights.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Dense career telemetry (status pipelines, feedback metrics, audio wave transcripts) structured through clean layered cards and purposeful reveals, minimizing cognitive friction.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in cold, technical slate, moving entirely away from hyper-saturated midnight blues toward neutral gunmetal and charcoal. High-contrast typography and radiant indigo accents guide focus without triggering visual fatigue during extended prep sessions.

### Core Tonal Hierarchy
- **Canvas / Root Background:** `#090d16` to `#0f172a` (deep gunmetal slate).
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Sidebar):** `#131c2e` / `#1e293b`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Cards & Active Modules):** `#1e293b` with semi-translucent alpha layers.
- **Surface Level 3 (Hover States & Elevated Popovers):** `#334155`.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle tier `#334155` (default), interactive tier `#475569` (hover/focus).

### Typography Tones
- **Primary Text:** `#f8fafc` (crisp, high legibility for headlines, key data, and transcripts).
- **Secondary Text:** `#94a3b8` (supporting metadata, pipeline stage tags, timestamps).
- **Muted / Placeholder:** `#64748b`.

### Accents & Telemetry
- **Primary Accent (`#6366f1`):** Key CTAs, primary active tab indicators, and progress tracks.
- **Secondary Accent (`#818cf8`):** Focus rings, interactive hover states, coaching highlight callouts.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#38bdf8`):** AI interview feedback metrics, live voice telemetry, and confidence scores.
- **Status Accents:**
  - *Applied / In Review:* `#818cf8` (Indigo)
  - *Interviewing:* `#38bdf8` (Cyan)
  - *Offer Received:* `#10b981` (Emerald)
  - *Archived / Rejected:* `#ef4444` (Crimson)

## Typography

Typography establishes an engineered, high-fidelity experience. **Geist** delivers pristine geometric neutrality across executive interfaces, eliminating the generic feel of ubiquitous neo-grotesques while preserving total clarity at small body sizes. **JetBrains Mono** is introduced for stage counters, time elapsed in mock interviews, salary metadata, and telemetry labels, reinforcing the product's analytical toolset.

### Typographic Principles
- **Monospace Pairing:** Reserve JetBrains Mono exclusively for status tags, audio timestamps, quantitative analytics, and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Negative Tracking:** Apply subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) on Geist headings to maintain typographic density and impact on slate backgrounds.
- **Contrast Ratios:** Avoid pure white for running body text to prevent glare; reserve `#f8fafc` for headlines and primary data points, routing explanatory paragraphs through `#94a3b8`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a modular 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate dense Kanban pipelines, mock interview video feeds, and multi-column telemetry sidebars.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Kanban transforms from horizontal multi-column to a segmented swipeable stage stack or dropdown filtered view. Coaching prompts stack beneath video/audio controls.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-pane view: collapsible navigation drawer with responsive split panels.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Fixed command sidebar (260px), multi-lane job board with drag handles, and docked interview simulator panel.

### Spacing Rules
Internal card padding aligns strictly to `space-md` (16px) for compact data cards and `space-lg` (24px) for parent workspace surfaces. Element gaps inside controls consistently use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
